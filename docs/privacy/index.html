Arrows

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Last updated 18 October 2026
Applies to the Android game Arrows (com.syedali.arrows)

Arrows is a puzzle game published by Syed Ali. This page explains what information the game and its advertising partner handle, and what choices you have.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no servers of ours.
  • Your progress, coins and settings stay on your own device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers and ad-interaction data so ads can be served and measured.
  • We never receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings at any time.

1.Who is responsible

The game is developed and published by Syed Ali, an independent developer, who is the data controller for anything this policy describes as collected by the game.

Contact for any privacy question or request: syedalisaleem14@gmail.com

2.Information stored on your device

Arrows saves the following locally, using Android's standard app storage:

  • your level progress and highest level reached;
  • your coin balance and remaining lives;
  • sound and haptic preferences.

This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

3.Information handled by advertising

Arrows displays ads supplied by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When the game requests or shows an ad, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier provided by Android;
  • IP address — used to deliver ads and to estimate a general region;
  • Device and app details — device model, operating system version, language, screen size, app version and similar technical information;
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were requested, shown or clicked, and when;
  • Diagnostic data — technical information about the ad request, used to fix errors and detect invalid traffic.

The game itself does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or payment details. There are no in-app purchases.

Ads require an internet connection. The game itself is playable offline; when there is no connection, no ad request is made.

4.How that information is used

  • to deliver the ads you see, including the optional rewarded videos you choose to watch;
  • to limit how often the same ad is shown and to measure ad performance;
  • to detect fraud, invalid traffic and abuse;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that affect your rights.

5.Sharing

We do not share information ourselves, because we do not hold any. Advertising data is processed by Google LLC and its partners under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • How AdMob handles data

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users, the developer or the public.

6.Your choices and rights

You are in control of the advertising identifier:

  • Reset or delete it — Android Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by device and Android version). Deleting it stops apps from using it to personalise ads.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising — same screen, or Google's Ads Settings.
  • Stop all collection by the game — uninstall it. Nothing remains on your device and no further ad requests are made.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, requests concerning advertising data should be directed to Google; we will still help where we can and will respond to any request sent to the address below.

Where the law requires consent for personalised advertising, the game shows Google's consent message on first launch, and that consent is what allows personalised ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the Android ad controls above.

7.Children

Arrows is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is configured with a maximum content rating of G so that creatives are suitable for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will act on it.

8.How long information is kept

We keep nothing, because we collect nothing. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention schedules, described in the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

9.Security

Ad requests are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the game is designed to hold as little data as possible: no account, no server, and no personal data of yours on our side.

10.International transfers

Google operates globally, so advertising data may be processed in countries other than yours. Google applies its own safeguards for those transfers, described in its privacy policy.

11.Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change — for example, if a new service is added — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
